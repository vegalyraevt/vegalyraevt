<section class="section-shell section-block music-workflow" aria-labelledby="music-workflow-title">
  <div class="music-copy"><p class="eyebrow">The creative process</p><h2 id="music-workflow-title">From a Melody to a Virtual Performance.</h2><p>A virtual singer still needs musical decisions: phrasing, expression, timing, editing, and a production that gives the voice somewhere to belong. Synthesizer V, Vocaloid, UTAU, and DAWs are tools in that process, not a one-click songwriting solution.</p><p>These are broad stages I work through, not a fixed sequence for every release. Original composition and arranging a cover begin from different material.</p></div>
  <ol class="music-workflow-list"><li><h3>Composition or Arrangement</h3><p>Write an original idea, or shape an arrangement around an existing song.</p></li><li><h3>Vocal Programming &amp; Tuning</h3><p>Edit notes, phrasing, timing, and expression to give the performance its character.</p></li><li><h3>Instrumentation &amp; Production</h3><p>Build the musical setting and make the parts work together.</p></li><li><h3>Mixing &amp; Finishing</h3><p>Balance the voice and instruments, refine the sound, and prepare the finished mix.</p></li><li><h3>Preparing for Release</h3><p>Bring the music, presentation, and appropriate credits together for publication.</p></li></ol>
</section>
